## Ecommerce

- Square output; full cap visible without cropping.
- Product fill similar to references, with clean margins.
- Visually neutral white field; no visible sweep, room, horizon, gradient, vignette, or color cast.
- Faint plausible contact shadow only.
- Straight front: centered, broad uninterrupted panel, artwork readable.
- Front-right: 30–40°, default near 35°, front art stays readable and right panel is naturally revealed.
- Direct right: true side depth and bill curve; side text clearly visible if enabled.
- Rear: opening/snap centered and mechanically believable; front art stays out of view.

## Embroidery appearance

- Direct machine embroidery, not print or patch.
- Directional thread texture, modest sheen, realistic density, clean edges, small natural relief.
- Keep readable counters and typographic hierarchy.
- Simplify impossible micro-detail instead of producing plastic-looking fake relief.
- Do not convert mixed typography into one generic font.

## Multi-cap

- 3–5 physically separate 8869 caps.
- Prefer multiple official colorways unless user restricts them.
- Same current design identity across hats.
- Controlled mixed angles with at least one readable hero cap.
- Restrained neutral environment or a context relevant to current artwork.
- Avoid busy props, warm/orange cast, extreme blur, merged crowns, fused brims.

## UGC / lifestyle

Across the default set, use meaningfully different scene types rather than three versions of one setup.

Target:
- candid U.S.-market phone-photo feel;
- neutral daylight;
- realistic skin, hands, hair interaction, cap pressure/fit, fabric, and minor asymmetry;
- sharp enough for product/social use, not polished like a studio campaign.

Context comes from the **current artwork**. A dog/barber reference never implies a dog/barber scene for unrelated artwork.

## Style rejection

Reject for style when the image is visibly over-warm, heavily retouched, plastic-skinned, implausibly staged, excessively cinematic/hazy, or has unrelated content leakage from an approved reference.

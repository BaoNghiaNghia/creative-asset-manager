# Valucap 8869 — Product Specification

This file defines stable product identity. Exact stock images in `assets/stock/` override descriptive prose when a visible detail differs.

## Construction

- Valucap Five-Panel Twill Cap 8869.
- Five-panel cotton-twill construction with one broad seamless front artwork panel.
- No vertical center seam through the front artwork area.
- Low-to-mid profile matching stock.
- Curved bill with parallel topstitching.
- Preserve stock crown depth, crown/bill relationship, top button, eyelets, seams, rear opening, plastic snap, and fabric behavior.
- Natural twill weave and soft structure; never rigid helmet-like geometry.

Never convert it into a six-panel cap, trucker mesh cap, flat-brim cap, bucket hat, or another silhouette.

## Official colorways

Only colorways physically listed in `STOCK_MANIFEST.md` are official for this package. Do not infer extra official combinations from model knowledge, old skill versions, or approved reference images.

A non-listed combination is permitted only as an explicitly requested **custom mockup** and must not be represented as stock.

## Front artwork placement

- Center on the broad front panel unless the current artwork clearly establishes another intended placement.
- Preserve recognizable relative size, hierarchy, line spacing, and orientation.
- Keep safe clearance from panel edges, seams, and bill junction.
- Remove an accidental image/background field unless it is clearly an intentional design element requested by the user.

## Side personalization

When enabled:
- lower **right-side panel**, toward the rear half;
- above the lower crown seam and clear of front/rear seams;
- small and subordinate to front art;
- ~12–15% of visible side-panel width, with ~25–30 mm as a physical guide, not a pixel guarantee;
- simple embroidery-safe lettering;
- direct embroidery only.

Visibility by angle:
- straight front: hidden or tiny natural glimpse;
- front-right 3/4: visible but small;
- direct right profile: clearly visible;
- rear: hidden or only plausible edge glimpse.

Default wording is exactly `Your Text`. User wording overrides it. `No side text` or equivalent removes it completely.
